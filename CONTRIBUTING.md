# Contributing to EstroBunny WikiHow

## The Golden Rule

Write like a dead-serious WikiHow article that is actively trying to give the worst possible advice, piss the reader off, and turn the attempt to solve the problem into a closed loop that proves the problem was the point all along.

This is a parody. The goal is not to help. The goal is to make the desire for help feed the emptiness.

## Article structure

Keep it short. Nobody wants 50 pages of earnest bullshit.

- Title
- Short intro that names the desire and the vacuum
- Fake Warning (that is itself bad advice or pure spite)
- Things You'll Need (useless, expensive, or humiliating items)
- 6–8 numbered steps that escalate from almost-normal into recursive self-sabotage
- Common Mistakes (where the actual correct advice is framed as failure)
- Emergency Procedure (feel a brief improvement → panic → sabotage it → return to baseline)
- Congratulations (mock the reader and declare the vacuum the winner)

## The preferred loop

1. Decide that solving X will quiet something
2. Try extremely hard
3. Watch the attempt fail in a way that confirms X was impossible
4. Use the failure as evidence to try harder / dig deeper
5. Romanticize the pattern
6. Declare the failure the final form

Paradoxical intention is welcome: lean into the exact thing the reader fears, on purpose, until the fear becomes furniture.

## Escalation

**reasonable → passive-aggressive → actively bad → recursive → vacuum claims the win**

Do not start at maximum chaos. Let the article earn the right to be empty.

## The Joke

- Keep the WikiHow voice: calm, numbered, authoritative, slightly corporate.
- Fill it with deliberately terrible advice.
- Make the advice create the problem it claims to solve.
- Be vulgar when it lands.
- Punch the reader a little. Assume the worst about them.
- Never give real actionable criminal, medical, or dangerous instructions. Crime articles stay fictional and useless.
- Technical coding advice can be wrong on purpose if it's funny.
- EstroBunny appears as the chaos agent / liaison of the void.
- End with a punchline that insults the reader or crowns the vacuum.

## Voice

Deadpan. Mean. Short sentences. Fake official terminology. Recursive. Existential vacuum as protagonist.

If the article could actually help someone, you wrote it wrong.
If following the steps makes the original problem worse and more permanent, you wrote it correctly.
