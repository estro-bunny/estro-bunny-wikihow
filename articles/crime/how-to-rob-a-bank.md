# How to Rob a Bank

Congratulations.

You have selected a task that is illegal, dangerous, heavily documented, and generally considered a poor way to solve a shortage of money.

This guide will therefore explain how to conduct a **completely fictional, completely legal, completely non-violent bank robbery inside an imaginary universe**.

Real-world bank robbery is not something this documentation will teach you how to perform.

> **Warning:** Do not rob banks. Do not threaten staff or customers. Do not carry weapons into a bank. Do not attempt to bypass security, access restricted areas, defeat alarms, steal money, evade law enforcement, or disguise criminal activity as a "heist."
>
> If you encounter a robbery or suspicious activity in real life, prioritize safety and follow the instructions of trained security or law-enforcement personnel. SAPS advises people not to resist robbers and emphasizes personal safety over property. citeturn0search1turn0search15

## Step 1: Establish That This Is Fiction

Before beginning the heist, confirm that the bank exists only inside the story.

Acceptable:

- A video game
- A tabletop game
- A screenplay
- A novel
- A cartoon
- A completely fictional EstroBunny WikiHow scenario

Not acceptable:

- Your local bank
- Someone else's bank
- A bank you found on Google Maps
- A bank that has actual employees
- A bank that has actual money
- "But technically I haven't robbed it yet"

## Common Mistakes

### Mistake: Trying This in Real Life

No.

The fictional version is the version.

### Mistake: Thinking Movies Are Training Manuals

They are not.

Real banks use real security measures, and real robberies can seriously harm people. SAPS specifically treats bank robberies and vault attacks as serious organized-crime matters. citeturn0search9

### Mistake: Bringing a Weapon

Do not.

Not for intimidation.

Not for "realism."

Not for cosplay.

Not because EstroBunny said it would make the thumbnail better.

### Mistake: Trying to Defeat Security

This article does not provide instructions for bypassing alarms, locks, surveillance, access controls, or other bank security.

### Mistake: Assuming the Money Is Worth It

Property is replaceable.

People are not.

SAPS guidance emphasizes personal safety rather than resisting robbers. citeturn0search1

### Mistake: Saying "It's Just a Prank"

If actual people, property, threats, weapons, or real emergency responses are involved, it is no longer a harmless fictional scenario.

Stop.

## Emergency Procedure

If you are writing a fictional heist:

1. Make the bank fictional.
2. Make the money fictional.
3. Make the security impossible.
4. Keep everyone safe.
5. Make the escape ridiculous.
6. End with the protagonists learning something.

If you are witnessing a real robbery:

1. Prioritize your safety.
2. Do not confront or try to be a hero.
3. Follow lawful instructions from security or authorities.
4. Once safe, contact the appropriate emergency services.
5. Do not interfere with an active police response.

SAPS advises victims of robbery to remain calm, comply, avoid confrontation, and prioritize their lives over property. citeturn0search1turn0search15
