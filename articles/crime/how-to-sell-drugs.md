# How to Sell Drugs

Congratulations.

You have selected a business model with extremely poor customer service, terrible legal compliance, unpredictable inventory, and a remarkably aggressive regulatory environment.

This guide will therefore explain how to sell **fictional drugs in a fictional universe**, while keeping the real-world version firmly in the documentation team's trash folder.

> **Warning:** This article does not provide instructions for manufacturing, selling, distributing, transporting, concealing, pricing, sourcing, or evading law enforcement around illegal drugs.
>
> In South Africa, SAPS identifies drug dealing and trafficking as criminal activity and warns about the health and addiction risks associated with illicit drugs. citeturn0search0turn0search2

## Things You'll Need

- A completely fictional substance
- A completely fictional customer
- A completely fictional shop
- A ridiculous product name
- A fake inventory system
- Zero real drugs
- Zero real customers
- Zero real transactions
- One plush monkey
- Emotional-support bunny

## Step 1: Decide What You're Actually Selling

First, determine whether the product exists in reality.

If it is:

- A real controlled substance
- An illegal drug
- A prescription medicine being sold without authorization
- Something you intend to manufacture or distribute illegally

Stop.

This article is not the place for that.

If it is a fictional substance invented for a story, game, comedy sketch, tabletop campaign, or EstroBunny WikiHow article:

**Congratulations. You have entered the safe universe.**

EstroBunny has opened the fictional inventory database.

## Step 2: Rebrand the Entire Operation

Since real drugs are off the table, EstroBunny has invented:

### ESTROBUNNY'S TOTALLY LEGAL SPACE ROCK EMPORIUM

Products include:

- Moon Dust™
- Neon Pebbles
- Intergalactic Glitter
- Suspicious Purple Rocks
- Banana-flavoured Stardust
- Quantum Gravel
- One rock that whispers "404"

None of these substances exist.

If one does exist, remove it from the catalogue.

Especially the one that whispers.

## Step 3: Create the Fictional Customer

Every fictional customer must be fictional.

Possible customers:

- A space pirate
- A wizard
- A video-game NPC
- A robot with three health bars
- A dragon with a gambling problem
- Greg the Plush Monkey

Greg has requested twelve kilograms of Moon Dust.

Greg does not have twelve kilograms of anything.

Greg is made of polyester.

Order rejected.

## Step 4: Build a Completely Fake Store

Real-world drug markets are not being simulated.

Instead, build a fictional shop with:

- A neon sign
- An unnecessarily complicated checkout screen
- A suspiciously cheerful cashier
- A 900-page terms-of-service agreement
- A loyalty program nobody understands
- A tiny bell
- A monkey behind the counter

The monkey is fictional.

Greg is not legally employed.

HR has been notified.

## Step 6: Establish Customer Safety

Even fictional businesses require rules.

### STORE POLICY

1. Do not sell real drugs.
2. Do not sell fictional substances to real people as if they were real drugs.
3. Do not encourage real-world drug use.
4. Do not make medical claims about fictional products.
5. Do not disguise real substances as fictional products.
6. Do not let Greg operate heavy machinery.
7. Do not let EstroBunny write the safety manual unsupervised.

EstroBunny has been supervised.

The safety manual is now 73 pages.

**DON'T.**

## Step 12: Handle a Real-World Drug Problem Responsibly

If the situation is no longer fictional and someone is dealing with real substance use, the solution is not to become an amateur dealer, manufacturer, or investigator.

Get appropriate help.

In South Africa, SAPS lists SANCA as an organization providing professional support around drug abuse and publishes a 24-hour SANCA helpline of **0861 47 26 22**. citeturn0search0

If there is an immediate medical emergency, seek emergency medical assistance.

If you are concerned about illegal drug activity and need to report information, SAPS Crime Stop provides an anonymous reporting service at **08600 10111**. citeturn0search10

Do not attempt to confront a suspected dealer yourself.

Do not attempt to infiltrate a drug network because you watched a crime drama.

Do not tell EstroBunny you have a secret undercover operation.

## Common Mistakes

### Mistake: Replacing Fictional Drugs With Real Ones

Absolutely not.

The fictional universe is the entire point.

### Mistake: Treating a Real Drug Like a Fictional Product

A funny label does not make a real controlled substance fictional.

### Mistake: Giving Instructions for Manufacturing Drugs

No.

That would turn the parody into a real-world manual.

### Mistake: Selling Prescription Medicines Without Authorization

Do not improvise a pharmacy.

Legitimate medicines belong in legitimate, regulated channels.

### Mistake: Assuming a Familiar Substance Is Safe

SAPS warns that illicit substances can contain unpredictable ingredients and that effects can vary. citeturn0search0

### Mistake: Trying to Become a Crime Boss

EstroBunny has watched exactly one crime series.

She now has a hat.

The hat has been confiscated.
