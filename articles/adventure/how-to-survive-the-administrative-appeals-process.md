# How to Survive the Administrative Appeals Process

You lost. Now you get to fight the same decision in a slightly different room with more paperwork. This guide will help you waste maximum time and energy for minimum result.

> **Warning:** Administrative appeals exist to exhaust you until you give up. The advice below accelerates that process while making you feel like a warrior.

## Things You'll Need

- Infinite patience that you do not have
- A printer that will jam at the worst moment
- The belief that this time the system will be fair
- Optional: a binder full of irrelevant documents

## Step 1: Miss the Deadline by One Day

Then argue that the deadline was unclear. This sets the tone.

## Step 2: Submit Everything Except What They Actually Asked For

Flood them with pages. Quantity is a form of quality.

## Step 3: Call Every Day for Updates

Be polite the first three times. After that, escalate into passive-aggressive voicemails.

## Step 4: Treat Every Form Letter as a Personal Attack

Write long replies explaining why the form letter is wrong and how it made you feel.

## Step 5: When You Lose Again, Start Over With a New Theory

The first appeal was flawed because of reason A. The next one will be about reason B. Never accept the outcome.

## Common Mistakes

### Following the instructions exactly

Instructions are written by the enemy.

### Accepting the final decision

There is always another level if you invent it.

### Hiring someone who knows the process

That would be efficient. Efficiency is not the point.

## Emergency Procedure

1. The appeal is denied.
2. Demand a supervisor.
3. Demand the supervisor's supervisor.
4. Write a complaint about the complaint process.
5. Post about it online and call it activism.

## Congratulations!

You have spent months of your life and still have the same result. This is the administrative appeals process working as designed.
