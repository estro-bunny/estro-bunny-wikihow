EstroBunny has made a mistake.

Not a small mistake.

Not a "forgot to submit the form" mistake.

> **Important:** This is a fictional EstroBunny WikiHow scenario, not legal advice. Contempt rules and procedures vary by jurisdiction and type of proceeding. In South African law, contempt can involve disobedience of a court order, with courts considering matters such as the existence of the order, notice or service, non-compliance, and whether the non-compliance was wilful or mala fide. citeturn0search12turn0search14turn0search15

The first rule is simple.

**Stop.**

If you are dealing with an actual legal matter, do not respond by:

- Destroying documents
- Ignoring further court orders
- Contacting people to pressure them
- Threatening anyone
- Posting your version of events online
- Inventing evidence
- Altering records
- Trying to intimidate the court
- Attempting to interfere with proceedings
- Opening a secret courtroom bunker

## Step 3: Locate the Original Order

In a contempt matter involving alleged disobedience of a court order, the actual order matters.

Find the document.

Read it.

Check what it required.

Check when it took effect.

Check who it applied to.

Check whether you received or knew about it.

Do not rely on your memory of what you think the judge said six months ago.

- What actions you took
- When you took them
- What remains outstanding
- Any communication relevant to compliance

Keep supporting records where appropriate.

Do not manufacture a timeline after the fact.

Use actual dates.

Use actual documents.

Use actual events.

## Step 5: Get Appropriate Legal Help

A contempt hearing can have serious consequences.

If this is a real matter, obtain qualified legal advice appropriate to the jurisdiction and proceeding.

Bring your documents.

Explain what happened honestly.

Tell your lawyer what you know, what you do not know, and what you have done since receiving the order.

Do not hide an inconvenient fact because you think it makes the story worse.

That usually makes the situation more complicated.

## Step 7: Understand the Difference Between Explanation and Excuse

If you failed to comply with an order, the circumstances matter.

Do not invent an excuse.

Do not exaggerate.

Do not minimize what happened without basis.

Explain the facts clearly and let your legal representative advise you on the legal implications.

In South African contempt jurisprudence, the requirements and evidentiary questions can depend on the nature of the contempt proceeding; official Justice sources describe the importance of the order, notice or service, non-compliance, and the circumstances surrounding whether the non-compliance was wilful or mala fide. citeturn0search12turn0search15

## Step 11: Do Not Contempt the Contempt Hearing

This sounds obvious.

Do not:

- Disobey a new order
- Interrupt proceedings
- Threaten participants
- Destroy evidence
- Refuse lawful directions simply to make a point
- Attempt to interfere with the court's functioning

## Emergency Procedure

If you discover a serious compliance problem while dealing with an actual court matter:

1. Stop making unnecessary changes.
2. Preserve relevant records.
3. Read the applicable order and documents carefully.
4. Contact qualified legal counsel or the appropriate legal service.
5. Explain the situation honestly.
6. Follow current lawful instructions.
7. Meet applicable deadlines where possible.
8. Document what you do.
9. Do not attempt retaliation or interference.
10. Do not open another terminal.
