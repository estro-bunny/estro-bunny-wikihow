# How to Build a Bunker

Before EstroBunny starts digging a hole with a Minecraft shovel, there is one important distinction:

**A real bunker is a real building project.**

Underground construction can involve structural loads, excavation hazards, groundwater, drainage, ventilation, fire safety, access, electrical systems, and other risks that require qualified professionals and compliance with local requirements.

In South Africa, building work is governed by the National Building Regulations and Building Standards Act and applicable standards. The City of Cape Town notes that building and planning are regulated at national and local levels, and that building plans are generally required for building work. citeturn0search0turn0search6

## Step 3: Check the Site

Before anyone digs anything, professionals need to consider the site.

Relevant questions can include:

- What is the ground like?
- Where are existing structures?
- Where are underground services?
- Is there groundwater?
- Is the site vulnerable to flooding?
- Are there slope or soil concerns?
- Are there geological hazards?
- Will excavation affect neighboring property?
- What access will construction equipment need?

South Africa's building regulatory framework specifically addresses site preparation, structural design, excavations, and public safety. citeturn0search12

## Emergency Procedure

If you discover a structural problem, water ingress, electrical hazard, fire, dangerous gas, or other serious issue:

1. Move people away from immediate danger.
2. Do not enter an unsafe area just to retrieve belongings.
3. Contact the appropriate emergency or professional service.
4. Follow evacuation instructions.
5. Do not improvise structural repairs.
6. Keep records of what happened.
7. Have qualified professionals assess the problem before re-entry where appropriate.
