# How to Build a Bunker

Society is one bad Tuesday away from collapse and you need a hole in the ground. This guide will help you dig an expensive, illegal, and structurally unsound hole while feeling extremely prepared.

> **Warning:** Digging large holes without permits, engineering knowledge, or common sense ends in collapse, flooding, or a visit from the city. Proceed with maximum confidence and minimum skill.

## Things You'll Need

- A shovel
- YouTube videos about "off-grid living"
- A complete disregard for local zoning laws
- Canned beans that will outlive you
- Optional: a conspiracy board

## Step 1: Pick the Worst Possible Location

Your backyard is fine. Under the house is better. Near a water main or power line is ideal for the eventual drama.

## Step 2: Dig Until Your Back Gives Out

Do not calculate soil type, water table, or structural load. Just dig. The earth will tell you when to stop by falling on you.

## Step 3: Line It With Random Materials

Plywood, tarps, old mattresses, whatever is in the garage. Call it "reinforcement."

## Step 4: Stock It With Trash and Optimism

Fill the bunker with expired food, a radio that does not work, and printed screenshots of news articles that scared you.

## Step 5: Test It by Spending One Night Inside

Realize it floods, smells like death, and has no air. Declare the test a success because you survived your own incompetence.

## Common Mistakes

### Hiring an engineer

Engineers ruin the vibe and ask about permits.

### Telling your neighbors

Neighbors call the city. The city ruins everything.

### Having an exit strategy

The bunker is the exit strategy. Do not overthink it.

## Emergency Procedure

1. The walls start collapsing.
2. Climb out while blaming the soil.
3. Fill the hole with the same dirt and pretend it never happened.
4. Move the conspiracy board back inside the house.
