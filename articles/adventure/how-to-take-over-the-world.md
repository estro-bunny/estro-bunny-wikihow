# How to Take Over the World

Excellent.

You have built the evil lair.

You have recruited the henchmen.

You have acquired the cape.

You have established a GitHub organization.

There is only one thing left to do.

**Take over the world.**

Fortunately, this is a fictional supervillain guide.

Real-world attempts to seize power, overthrow governments, attack people, sabotage infrastructure, manipulate elections, or otherwise harm people are not part of this article.

Instead, we will construct the greatest fictional world-domination plan ever devised by a bunny with too many terminals open.

World domination is a classic supervillain trope precisely because it is absurdly ambitious. Fiction frequently treats it as the ultimate villain objective, while stories also enjoy the joke that actually ruling everything would be considerably more complicated than shouting "I have won!" from a throne. citeturn0search0turn0search4

> **Warning:** All governments, populations, infrastructure, henchmen, headquarters, world maps, and domination technology appearing in this guide are fictional. Do not use this article as a blueprint for real-world wrongdoing.

## Step 1: Define Your Goal

A proper supervillain needs a clear objective.

Possible fictional goals include:

- Rule the world
- Make everything pink
- Replace every computer wallpaper with bunny art
- Force all terminals to use Comic Sans
- Establish mandatory snack breaks

## Common Mistakes

### Mistake: Making the Plan Actually Harmful

Do not.

Keep world domination fictional.

The joke works better when the final superweapon is a CSS file.

### Mistake: Forgetting the Administration

Congratulations.

You won.

Now answer 400 emails.

### Mistake: Building Too Much Infrastructure

EstroBunny has created:

- 14 servers
- 6 dashboards
- 9 repositories
- 3 documentation portals
- 2 status pages
- 1 spreadsheet
- 47 tabs in that spreadsheet

She has forgotten the original password.

### Mistake: Assuming Everyone Wants Your Evil Empire

People can simply decline.

That is why this fictional takeover happens through voluntarily used software rather than coercion.

### Mistake: Forgetting Version Control

EstroBunny has committed directly to main.

The documentation team has entered a state of spiritual exhaustion.

## Emergency Procedure

If your fictional world-domination plan becomes too complicated:

1. Stop.
2. Close the command center.
3. Save the project.
4. Document the current state.
5. Back up important data.
6. Ask the team what is actually necessary.
7. Delete the unnecessary laser spreadsheet.
8. Eat a snack.
9. Reconsider whether world domination was a reasonable project goal.
10. Open-source the harmless parts.

If the fictional villain says:

> "I have a backup plan."

Ask:

> "Is it documented?"

If the answer is no:

**There is no backup plan.**

There is only panic with branding.
