# How to Survive Jury Duty

You have been summoned to sit in a room with strangers and decide someone else's fate while getting paid less than a teenager at a drive-thru. This guide will help you get dismissed or make the experience worse for everyone.

> **Warning:** Lying to the court can get you in actual trouble. The advice below is for people who want to be annoying rather than criminal.

## Things You'll Need

- A strong opinion about everything
- The inability to keep your mouth shut
- A phone you will keep checking
- Optional: a conspiracy theory about the justice system

## Step 1: Answer Every Voir Dire Question Too Honestly

When they ask if you can be fair, say no. When they ask if you have biases, list them all. Volunteer information no one requested.

## Step 2: Make Eye Contact With the Defendant and Smile Creepily

This makes everyone uncomfortable. Comfortable jurors get selected.

## Step 3: Ask "Clarifying" Questions That Are Actually Rants

Turn every simple question into a speech about society, media, or your personal philosophy.

## Step 4: If Selected, Be the Juror From Hell

Argue about every piece of evidence. Refuse to deliberate efficiently. Bring up unrelated personal stories. Hold out until everyone hates you.

## Step 5: Leak Details to Your Group Chat

Post vague updates that are somehow still too specific. When the judge finds out, claim free speech.

## Common Mistakes

### Being normal and quiet

Normal people get stuck on long trials.

### Taking the civic duty seriously

Seriousness is how they get you.

### Not having a medical excuse prepared

Always have a vague back issue ready.

## Emergency Procedure

1. You are selected anyway.
2. Fake a coughing fit.
3. Ask to speak to the judge privately.
4. Overshare until they dismiss you out of exhaustion.

## Congratulations!

You either got out of jury duty or became a cautionary tale for the next panel.
