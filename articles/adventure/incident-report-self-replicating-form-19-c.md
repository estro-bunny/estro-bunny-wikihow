# EstroBunny Incident Report: The Self-Replicating Form 19-C

**Incident ID:** EB-ADMIN-19C  
**Severity:** Bureaucratically Critical  
**Status:** Contained-ish  
**Location:** EstroBunny Administrative Appeals Department

## Summary

During an administrative appeal, EstroBunny encountered **Form 19-C**, a document believed to be responsible for generating additional paperwork whenever its instructions were followed.

The incident escalated after three contradictory instructions were discovered:

- **Form 19-C:** “Attach all supporting documents.”
- **Form 19-C(a):** “Do not attach documents already held by the department.”
- **Form 19-C(b):** “Form 19-C(a) must be submitted with Form 19-C.”

Further investigation revealed that Form 19-C(a) could not be completed without information contained in Form 19-C(b), while Form 19-C(b) could not be submitted without Form 19-C(a).

At this point, the paperwork had achieved **bureaucratic recursion**.

## Containment Attempt

The Documentation Team implemented the following emergency controls:

1. Stop creating copies of Form 19-C.
2. Stop renaming forms `FINAL_FINAL_ACTUAL`.
3. Freeze all unnecessary submissions.
4. Preserve the original documents.
5. Identify the authoritative instructions.
6. Request clarification from the appropriate authority.
7. Place Form 19-C in a sealed folder labelled:

`DO_NOT_DUPLICATE_UNTIL_CLARIFIED`

EstroBunny immediately attempted to photocopy the folder.

The Documentation Team physically removed the photocopier.

## Final Status

No additional forms were created.

EstroBunny has been prohibited from naming any future administrative document `Form 19-D`.
