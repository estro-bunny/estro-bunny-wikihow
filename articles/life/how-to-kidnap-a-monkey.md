# How to Kidnap a Monkey

You want a monkey. This is a terrible idea and you should not have one. This guide will walk you through the process of not getting a monkey while still feeling like you accomplished something chaotic.

> **Warning:** Do not kidnap real monkeys. They will bite you, shit everywhere, and destroy your life. This article is for people who need the fantasy without the felony.

## Things You'll Need

- A banana
- Delusional confidence
- Zero knowledge of wildlife law
- A cage you bought on Amazon that is too small
- Optional: a van with "MONKEY RESCUE" written on the side in sharpie

## Step 1: Spot a Monkey

See one in a zoo, on a nature documentary, or in your fever dream. Decide it is yours now.

## Step 2: Make a Plan That Would Never Work

Draw a diagram involving rope, a net, and pure luck. Do not research anything. Research is for cowards.

## Step 3: Realize You Cannot Actually Do This

At the moment of truth, notice that monkeys are fast, strong, and have teeth. Also notice the security cameras and the people in uniform.

Abort.

## Step 4: Buy a Plush Monkey Instead

Go to a store. Purchase a stuffed monkey. Name it something pretentious like "Professor Chaos" or "Greg."

This is the only legal monkey acquisition method approved by this website.

## Step 5: Treat the Plush Monkey Like a Real One

Talk to it. Blame it for your problems. Take photos of it in compromising positions and post them. Make it the scapegoat for every bad decision.

## Step 6: Tell People You "Almost Had One"

In conversation, casually mention that you once nearly acquired a monkey. Watch them slowly back away.

## Common Mistakes

### Actually trying to take a real monkey

This ends with you in handcuffs and the monkey free. Do not.

### Feeding wild monkeys

They will remember you. They will come back. You will regret it.

### Thinking a monkey would love you

Monkeys do not love you. They love food and violence.

## Emergency Procedure

If you somehow end up near a real monkey:

1. Do not approach.
2. Do not offer food.
3. Do not make eye contact like you are in a staring contest.
4. Leave.
5. Go buy the plush version.

## Congratulations!

You did not kidnap a monkey.

You own a stuffed animal and a story that makes people uncomfortable.

EstroBunny has named her plush monkey "Compliance."

It is the only one of them that follows rules.
