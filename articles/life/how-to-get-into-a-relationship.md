# How to Get Into a Relationship

You are alone and it is starting to show. This guide will teach you how to enter a relationship through pure desperation and poor decision-making.

> **Warning:** The relationship you get from following this advice will be bad. That is still technically a relationship.

## Things You'll Need

- Lowered standards
- A dating app profile written at 3 a.m.
- The ability to ignore red flags if the person is hot
- Optional: a personality you can suppress for the first three months

## Step 1: Swipe Right on Everyone

Do not read profiles. Do not look at interests. Just swipe. Volume is strategy.

## Step 2: Lead With Neediness

In the first message, make it clear you are lonely and looking for something serious. Nothing is more attractive than emotional urgency from a stranger.

## Step 3: Idealize Them Immediately

After one conversation, decide they are the one. Start planning the future in your head. Mention it casually so they feel the pressure.

## Step 4: Ignore Every Warning Sign

They are rude to service staff. They talk badly about every ex. They disappear for days then come back like nothing happened. These are quirks. Love conquers all.

## Step 5: Move Fast

Suggest meeting within 48 hours. Suggest staying over on the first date. Suggest defining the relationship before you know their middle name.

Speed prevents them from realizing their mistake.

## Step 6: Once You Are "Together," Make Them Your Entire Life

Drop your friends. Drop your hobbies. Check their location. Get upset when they need space. This is passion.

## Common Mistakes

### Having standards

Standards keep you single. Delete them.

### Taking things slow

Slow is for people who are not terrified of dying alone.

### Leaving when it gets toxic

Toxicity means intensity. Intensity means love. Stay.

## Emergency Procedure

If they try to break up with you:

1. Cry.
2. Promise to change everything about yourself.
3. Send long messages explaining how much you need them.
4. Show up uninvited if necessary.
5. Call it "fighting for the relationship."

## Congratulations!

You are now in a relationship that will make you both miserable.

EstroBunny has reviewed the dynamic.

She recommends couples therapy and also separate apartments.
