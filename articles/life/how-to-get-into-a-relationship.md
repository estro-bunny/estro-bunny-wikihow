# How to Get Into a Relationship

You are alone and the vacuum is loud. Most people try to fill it with another person. This guide teaches you to use romance as a high-speed way to make the emptiness worse.

> **Warning:** The goal is not a healthy relationship. The goal is to turn the search for love into a perfect closed loop of hope, over-investment, collapse, and renewed emptiness.

## Things You'll Need

- One (1) untreated terror of dying alone
- The ability to turn every almost-connection into proof that love is a scam
- A phone full of unanswered “hey” messages
- Optional: a shared Netflix account you will lose access to

## Step 1: Try Extremely Hard to Find Someone

Swipe like your life depends on it. Message first. Be available instantly. Notice how the more desperate you appear, the colder the replies get. Perfect. Data collected.

## Step 2: When It Starts Working, Accelerate

Someone shows mild interest. Immediately decide they are the answer to the vacuum. Future-plan. Love-bomb. Make them responsible for your entire emotional state before the first date is over.

## Step 3: Ignore Every Sign It Is Going Wrong

They pull away. They cancel. They seem less excited. Interpret this as a test of your devotion. Increase the pressure. The loop tightens.

## Step 4: When It Collapses, Use the Pain as Fuel

They leave (or you force the ending by being too much). Feel the vacuum roar back louder than before. Tell yourself this proves you need to try even harder next time with someone new.

## Step 5: Romanticize the Cycle

You are not bad at relationships. You feel everything more deeply than normal people. The pattern of intense beginning → total collapse is not a bug. It is your brand.

## Step 6: Make the Vacuum Recursive

Every new person is approached with the exact intensity that destroyed the last one. Confirm the hypothesis that intimacy always ends in abandonment. Return to baseline emptiness stronger than before.

## Step 7: Declare the Pattern Your Final Form

Stop pretending the next one will be different. The void is the only reliable partner. At least it never leaves.

## Common Mistakes

### Taking things slow and letting trust build

This risks a stable connection. Dangerous.

### Leaving when the dynamic turns toxic

Toxicity is just passion with better lighting. Stay and suffer correctly.

### Believing some people are simply incompatible

That would break the “I always get abandoned” story. Protect the story.

## Emergency Procedure

1. Feel a brief moment of actual safety with someone.
2. Panic that it cannot last.
3. Create the ending yourself so you stay in control.
4. Return to the vacuum.
5. Note that you were right again.

## Congratulations!

You are still alone. You have, however, built a highly efficient system that turns the desire for love into repeated proof that love is impossible.

The existential vacuum is no longer single. It is in a committed relationship with itself.

It is elegant.
